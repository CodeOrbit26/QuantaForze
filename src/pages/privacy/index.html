Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026 | Version: v2.4.0

1. Zero-Trust Local Data Ownership

At QuantaForze Technologies Inc., privacy is built directly into our execution architecture. Source code, local repository files, and workspace states analyzed by QuantaForze CLI and SDK remain strictly on your local machine or dedicated enterprise tenant. We do not transmit or index your source code for global model training.

2. AxioVital Healthcare Compliance (HIPAA & GDPR)

The AxioVital Connected Operating Environment complies with HIPAA and GDPR regulations. Patient profiles, clinical records, and inter-hospital care continuity channels use end-to-end TLS 1.3 and AES-256-GCM encryption with explicit role-based access control.

3. Explicit Human-in-the-Loop Approval

Autonomous AI agents managed by QuantaForze require explicit human authorization before performing high-impact operations such as modifying databases, initiating financial transfers, or altering system configurations.

4. Contact Security Officer

For privacy verifications or security audits, contact Chief Security Officer Yash Kushwaha at security@quantaforze.com.
