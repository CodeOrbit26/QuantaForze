Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

At QuantaForze, privacy and security are not secondary features — they are fundamental architectural requirements across our platforms.

1. Core Privacy Principles

QuantaForze operates under strict data minimization and zero-trust security controls. Autonomous agents and platforms (including AxioVital) require explicit authorization before accessing sensitive files, devices, or healthcare records.

Key Commitments:

  • Local Data Ownership: Source code, user files, and private workspace artifacts remain strictly on your local machine or dedicated enterprise tenant.
  • Explicit Human Oversight: High-impact file mutations, network transfers, and clinical actions require human approval by default.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Device-to-device communications via QuantaForze Connect are secured using TLS 1.3 and AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Audit Logging: All agent execution steps generate immutable local audit trails for complete transparency.

2. Healthcare Data Compliance (AxioVital)

AxioVital complies with global healthcare data protection standards, including HIPAA and GDPR. Patient information transfer across inter-hospital continuity channels is cryptographically signed and logged.
