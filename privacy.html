Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

🛡️
Privacy by Design: QuantaForge systems process user intent and data under explicit permission controls. We do not sell your personal data or retain unauthorized clinical information.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with QuantaForge platforms (including AxioVital, QuantaForge Autonomous Agents, and the Intelligence Layer), we collect:

  • Account Data: Email address, organization details, and authentication credentials.
  • System Telemetry: Anonymized performance logs, execution trajectories, and tool call latency.
  • Permissions Context: User-granted scope configurations for cross-device automation.

2. Healthcare Data & AxioVital Security

AxioVital operates under strict HIPAA and ISO/IEC 27001 compliance standards. Patient data is encrypted end-to-end both in transit and at rest using AES-256 and TLS 1.3.

3. Autonomous Agent Permissions

QuantaForge Autonomous Agents execute file transfers and device operations strictly following explicit user authorization. Every action is recorded in an immutable local audit log.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@quantaforge.com.
